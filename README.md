## A World Where Every Choice Matters

**Eldanior** transforms Hytale into a full-scale MMORPG with deep progression, a living economy, political intrigue and ruthless PvP. Whether you rise as a feared Warrior, a cunning Merchant prince, or an all-powerful Archmage — your story is yours to write.

## Deep Combat System

Every fight is tactical. Every stat matters.

- **6 Core Stats** — Strength, Vitality, Intelligence, Endurance, Agility, Luck
- **+3 Attribute Points per level** — Full control over your build
- **Critical Hits** — Luck-based with scaling bonuses up to 20%+
- **Parry System** — Block 15%-45% damage with passive skill investment
- **Dodge System** — Complete evasion from 3% to 25% chance
- **Damage Reduction** — Flat and percentage-based, stackable
- **Kill Streaks** — Track your rampage, compete for the best streak

### Mob Scaling

Monsters scale with your level. Every zone stays relevant, every fight stays dangerous. Virtual HP pools, dynamic damage, and XP rewards that match the challenge.

**9 Mob Factions** — Outlanders, Kweebecs, Elementals, Trorks, Slothians, Void Creatures, Undead, Animals, and more.

## Feudal Society & Political Intrigue

### Nobility System — 7 Ranks

Rise through the ranks of a **feudal hierarchy** with real political power:

| Rank | Title | Privilege |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Roturier (Commoner) | — |
| 2 | Chevalier (Knight) | Promote commoners |
| 3 | Baron | Small court |
| 4 | Comte (Count) | Tax collection rights |
| 5 | Duc (Duke) | Territory control |
| 6 | Marquis | Regional authority |
| 7 | Roi (King) | Supreme ruler |

Higher ranks can **promote vassals**, creating a living chain of allegiance. Each rank grants a **Dignity Aura** — a visible aura that intimidates and slows nearby enemies.

### 13 Noble Families

Pledge your loyalty to one of **13 noble houses**, each with unique bonuses:

- **Royal:** Eldanior
- **Marquis:** Luminara, Valmontis, Runkandel, Zippel
- **Duchy:** Goldcrest, Warbane, Ironveil, Spellweave, Nighthollow, Silkroad, Frostguard, Swiftquiver

Family treasuries, contribution tracking, and inter-family rivalry fuel the political game.

### 480+ Titles

Earn and display **480+ unique titles** based on your achievements, class, combat record, and social standing. Titles update in real-time as you meet conditions.

### Church System

A parallel hierarchy of faith — climb from Laique to Cardinal through devotion and service.

## Built for Communities

### Server Administration

Full admin toolkit built in:

- **Admin GUI** — Real-time server dashboard with player stats, active duels, economy overview
- **Admin Commands** — Set levels, give XP, manage gold, teleport, manage parcels
- **Leaderboards** — Track top players across multiple categories
- **Hologram System** — Place information displays anywhere in the world

### Performance Optimized

- **Optimized combat loops** — No unnecessary object cloning or heavy logging in hot paths
- **Memory management** — Automatic cleanup of player data on disconnect
- **Atomic file writes** — Data never corrupts, even on crashes
- **Backup system** — Automatic `.bak` files for all persistent data
- **Tick-throttled systems** — ECS systems run at controlled intervals to prevent lag

### Persistence

Everything saves. Everything persists across restarts:

- Player stats, skills, inventory, quests, cooldowns
- Guild data, family affiliations, nobility ranks
- Territory ownership, treasury balances, tax records
- Shop listings, pending offline earnings
- Hologram positions, arena configs
